THE 3 R's

Three habits. One greener campus.

Every challenge on Fun Collectors starts with one of these

Reduce

Cut down on single-use plastic and wasted energy, and earn XP for every small change.

Reuse

Bring your own bottle, bag or container, and complete challenges that keep items out of the bin.

Recycle

Sort your waste properly, snap a photo as proof, and turn it into points you can redeem.

Behind every number is a student who chose a greener habit. Together, APU is turning small daily actions into real change on campus.

GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about APU Green Badge.

What is APU Green Badge?

APU Green Badge encourages students to participate in sustainable activities and help build a greener campus.

How do I earn points and XP?

Choose a challenge, complete the required activity, and submit photo evidence for verification to earn points and XP.

How do I earn badges?

Complete eligible sustainability challenges to unlock badges and track your achievements.

Can I redeem my points for rewards?

Yes. You can use your accumulated points to redeem available rewards listed on the platform.

How is my submitted photo verified?

An administrator reviews your submitted photo and checks whether it meets the requirements of the challenge.
